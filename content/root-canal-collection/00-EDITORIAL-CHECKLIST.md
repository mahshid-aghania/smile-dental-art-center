# Editorial Checklist — Items Requiring Clinic Confirmation & Clinical Review

Every article ships with a per-article "Editorial notes" block. This master checklist consolidates the recurring items the clinic must resolve **before publication**.

## 1. Clinical review required (dentist sign-off before publishing)
Articles containing symptom-triage, recovery expectations, red-flags, or medication discussion must be reviewed by Dr. Neda Kadivar (or a qualified dentist) and only then may carry any "reviewed by" label — **and only if a real review occurred**. Until confirmed, each such article's notes read *"Clinical review required before publication."*

Priority clinical-review set: **RC04, RC05, RC13, RC15, RC24, RC31, RC33, RC36, RC38, RC41, RC43, RC44, RC46, RC49** (urgent symptoms, systemic-risk, first-aid, recovery, medication-adjacent, pregnancy). All other clinical articles also require review; these are the highest-risk.

## 2. "Medically/clinically reviewed" labelling
Do **not** attach "Medically reviewed by Dr. Neda Kadivar" to any article unless a documented review has taken place. Default state = unlabelled + note in editorial block.

## 3. No individualized medication dosing
Articles mention over-the-counter pain relief only in general terms ("as directed on the label / as advised by your dentist or pharmacist"). No doses, frequencies, or drug-specific regimens. Confirm this held in RC33, RC36, RC31, RC25, RC49.

## 4. CDCP accuracy (do NOT reuse the implant exclusion)
RC20 (and CDCP mentions in RC18, RC22, RC42) state that the CDCP **does** cover root canal treatment/pulpectomies for eligible patients, that **retreatment requires preauthorization**, and that co-payments depend on income. Re-verify against canada.ca at publication. This is the opposite of the implant situation — do not cross-contaminate.

## 5. Fees, prices, discounts, financing — clinic must supply or omit
No article states a dollar figure, price range, discount, or financing term. Cost articles (RC18, RC19, RC23) explain *drivers* and direct readers to request a written estimate via `/root-canal-markham`. If the clinic wants real figures, it must supply and approve them (and note the Ontario fee-guide basis and effective year).

## 6. Clinic-specific capability claims — confirm or remove
The following must be **confirmed true** by the clinic before any article implies them; otherwise keep the generic/educational framing already written:
- Sedation modalities offered (RC29, RC47) — currently written as *general education*, not a clinic offering.
- Whether the clinic performs retreatment / surgery in-house or refers (RC30, RC42, RC43) — currently written as *general pathways*.
- 3D imaging / CBCT, microscopes, rubber dam, or other equipment (RC07, RC24) — currently written generically; the live site mentions "digital X-rays and 3D imaging," which may be cited only if still accurate.
- Same-day treatment / after-hours / emergency capacity (RC04, RC44, RC49) — **not claimed**; articles link to `/emergency-dentist-markham` and advise calling. Confirm the emergency page's stated availability.
- Years of experience with endodontics, success rates, specialist status — **not claimed** anywhere. Dr. Kadivar is referred to as a general dentist; do not upgrade to endodontist/specialist.
