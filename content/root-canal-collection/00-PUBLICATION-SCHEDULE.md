# Prioritized Publication Schedule — Root Canal Collection

**Basis:** content readiness + internal-link dependencies (hubs before spokes) + commercial/booking value. **This is a sequencing plan, not a ranking forecast.** Google needs time to process new content; no timing of ranking effects is promised.

**Dependency principle:** publish hub articles before the spokes that link up to them, and publish articles before other articles that link *to* them. All links to existing **[E]** pages are safe from day one.
